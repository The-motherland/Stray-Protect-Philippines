About SPP

Learn about our mission, objectives, and public protective services.

Our Mission

Stray Protect Philippines (SPP) serves as a digital reporting node and public education repository dedicated to curbing animal cruelty, abandonment, and illegal wildlife exploitation throughout the archipelago.

Core Goals

1. Facilitate direct citizen reporting to the Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI) and DENR.
2. Educate the public on Philippine Republic Acts regarding animal rights.
3. Promote humane community management for stray animals.

Our Services

• Incident Logging & Automated Dispatch
• Legal Flipbook & Statutory Resources
• Educational Advocacy Media Distribution
• Emergency Contacts Referral Network

Disclaimer

"Stray Protect Philippines (SPP) is an independent, citizen-led open-source project. We are not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI) or any government agency. This platform serves as a public directory tool to help citizens route animal cruelty reports directly to the proper legal authorities."
